# Official organization logos

Assets are hosted locally to avoid external image requests. JPG versions of HUST and Cobot are displayed with their own white backgrounds and without color filters. Xidian uses the emblem portion of its combined mark. Osaka retains its blue backing for the official white emblem. These identify the education and employment entries on the author's personal CV.

- `osaka.svg`: https://www.osaka-u.ac.jp/assets/images/footer/logo.svg — The University of Osaka's official ginkgo mark.
- `hust.jpg`: https://img.gaoxiaojob.com/uploads/company_logo/20230210090011_77961.jpg — color university emblem from the HUST recruitment profile, consistent with the official visual identity at https://vi.hust.edu.cn/jcbf/bzgf/xhgf.htm. Downloaded unchanged as JPEG.
- `xidian.png`: https://www.xidian.edu.cn/2020images/logo-on.png — Xidian official homepage mark; only the emblem at the left is displayed.
- `huawei.png`: https://www.huawei.com/-/media/htemplate-home/1.0.1.20251205144752/components/assets/img/apple-touch-icon-180.png — Huawei official site icon.
- `cobot.jpg`: https://pic2.pedaily.cn/stockcompany/202212/no/20221222369.jpg — company logo from the Cobot profile at https://m.pedaily.cn/vc/company/22369.html. The source URL returns PNG bytes; exported as RGB JPEG on a white background without changing the logo.

Organization names, homepages and asset paths are maintained in `_data/organizations.yml`.
